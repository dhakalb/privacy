Stickybeak Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Stickybeak does not collect, store or sell your personal data. The subscriptions, prices, dates and notes you enter stay on your device.

Data you enter

Everything you add to Stickybeak is stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive it. If you delete the app, or use Settings → Delete all data, it is removed. iOS may include app data in your own encrypted iCloud or device backups, which we cannot access.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications by iOS on your device. No reminder content is sent to us or any third party.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app checks Apple's signed purchase records on your device to unlock Stickybeak Pro. We do not use any third-party billing or analytics service, and we never see your payment details.

Security

Your data is stored in an encrypted database on your device. The encryption key is kept in the iOS Keychain. You can also turn on a Face ID lock in Settings, which hides the app's contents in the app switcher.

No tracking, no ads

Stickybeak contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no third-party tracking.

Children

Stickybeak is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
