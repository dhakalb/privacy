Common questions

I'm not getting reminders

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Stickybeak and make sure notifications are allowed. Then open Stickybeak once so it can reschedule your reminders.

Does Stickybeak cancel subscriptions for me?

No. Stickybeak shows step-by-step instructions and opens the right page, but only you can cancel with the provider. Marking a subscription as cancelled in Stickybeak only updates the app.

How do I cancel Stickybeak Pro?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Stickybeak and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage or cancel subscription inside the app.

I got a new iPhone. How do I restore Pro?

Open Stickybeak → Settings → See plans → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. See our privacy policy.

Are you connected to the services and banks shown in the app?

No. Stickybeak is independent and not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any service, bank or card issuer. Names and icons are shown only to help you recognise your own payments, and remain the property of their owners.
